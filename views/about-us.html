Our story

In 2007, N. S. V. S. Prasad opened Sree Datta Sports in Tirupati as a tennis and sports academy. The academy coached Indian players and supported their development. He later started Likitha Sports Surface & Sports Suppliers to build and maintain the places where people train and compete.

We install sports surfaces for stadiums, clubs, schools, universities, gymnasiums, hotels, defence establishments and corporate facilities. Our brochure identifies the company as ISO 9001:2015 certified.

Construction and renovation

  • New court construction, court repairs and renovation
  • Court resurfacing and painting, and surface spraying for athletic tracks
  • Indoor and outdoor swimming-pool construction
  • Indoor and outdoor gym equipment supply
  • Civil works, drainage, fencing, lighting and paving

Materials, installation and warranty

Our teams install wooden and acrylic synthetic surfaces using directly sourced materials. We use Lean and Six Sigma practices to manage cost, quality and installation time. We consider player comfort, human health and environmental safety when selecting materials. Our all-weather surfaces are designed for consistent bounce and spin and ease of maintenance.

Our brochure lists project warranties of 1 to 5 years, depending on the work and the climatic conditions at the site.

Courts and tracks

Tennis courts

We lay acrylic synthetic Pace Court surfaces on asphalt or concrete. The brochure lists 5-, 6-, 8-, 10- and 12-layer options. Cushion thickness depends on the level of play and budget.

The 8-layer system uses a primer, resurfacer, three cushion layers, a synthetic paint base coat and two top coats. The 5-layer system uses a primer, resurfacer, base coat and two top coats. We offer US-imported acrylic for indoor and outdoor use, perimeter chain-link fencing and lighting to international tennis federation norms.

Basketball courts

For indoor courts, we install air-cushioned wooden floors with kiln-dried, FSC-certified imported oak, teak, maple or beech boards. The brochure lists fir, spruce and pine for base frames and describes a DIN-certified anti-skid finish. This flooring also serves multipurpose halls, badminton and squash courts, and gymnasiums.

We also install 5- and 8-layer acrylic synthetic systems on asphalt or concrete using US-imported material. Services include civil construction, arena accessories, chain-link fencing and lighting to international basketball federation norms.

Badminton courts

Indoor air-cushioned floors use kiln-dried, FSC-certified oak, teak, maple or beech boards over a pine frame with an anti-skid finish. Acrylic synthetic flooring uses a sports mat over concrete with cushioning selected for the players and budget. PVC vinyl flooring is available in different brands and thicknesses for existing concrete or wooden floors.

We also supply arena accessories and undertake civil construction, chain-link fencing and lighting.

Squash courts

Our brochure describes a World Squash Federation-approved free-floating wooden floor. The air-cushioned system uses kiln-seasoned or dried American hard maple or European sycamore boards, pine runners and a 20 mm cushion pad.

White rebound hard-plaster walls provide ball visibility and a predictable rebound. A 12 mm toughened-glass back wall gives spectators and coaches a clear view. The door hinge is designed to prevent finger trapping.

Volleyball courts, jogging tracks and running tracks

We apply acrylic synthetic flooring over asphalt or concrete, with cushion thickness selected for player level and budget. To convert a concrete hard court, we bond a sports mat with polymer before applying an 8-layer acrylic cushion system. We also provide fencing, lighting and stormwater drainage.

Other installations include artificial turf for football, children’s play areas and cricket net-practice pitches, rubber flooring for play areas and gyms, and poles, nets and playing equipment.

Swimming pools

Likitha Swimming Pools builds, remodels and maintains pools in India. Our team helps clients choose a design, construction system and maintenance approach suited to the site and budget.

Choose a pool design

  • Infinity pools for a visually striking edge.
  • Overflow pools for a scenic setting.
  • Skimmer pools for an elegant, cost-effective design.
  • Cardio pools for clients seeking quality within budget.

Choose a construction system

iPanel liner pools use a panel system and PVC liner. The brochure describes the panel as patented and the liner as leak-free. Composite pools use fibreglass, come in various shapes and sizes, and are described in the brochure as an eco-friendly, cost-effective choice.

Equipment and coaching

  • Surfaces and lighting. Artificial grass turf, synthetic acrylic turf, EPDM flooring, rubber and gym flooring, PVC flooring, SBR flooring and floodlights.
  • Mesh and nets. GI and MS coated mesh, cricket, football and tennis nets, fencing nets, anti-bird nets, safety nets and shade nets.
  • Equipment and apparel. Indoor and open-gym equipment, sports and children’s play equipment, printed sports apparel, sports branding and printing, and trophies.
  • Coaching. Tennis, basketball, football, cricket, table tennis, swimming, badminton, chess, martial arts, skating and pickleball. We also offer school sports management.
